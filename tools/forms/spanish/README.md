# Spanish CDSS forms — downloaded manually 2026-08-22

cdss.ca.gov blocks automated fetches (302 to a dead host), so these came from a browser.

| file | what it is | useful for |
|---|---|---|
| `cf377_6_sp.pdf` | **CF 377.6 (SP)** — Notificación de Acción, Se Necesita Información/Verificación. Rev 8/13. | Exactly the Spanish CF 377.6. Notice-level vocabulary: `NOTIFICACIÓN DE ACCIÓN`, `SE NECESITA INFORMACIÓN/VERIFICACIÓN`, `Fecha de la notificación`, `Nombre del caso`, `Número del caso`, `Nombre del trabajador`, `Número del trabajador`, `Número de teléfono`, `Horario del trabajador`, `Dirección`. |
| `cf30_sp_sar7-reminder.pdf` | **CF 30 (SP)** — Aviso de Recordatorio, SAR 7. *Not the SAR 7 form itself* — it's the reminder that one is missing. | Official Spanish name for the form: **"Reporte sobre el estado de elegibilidad (SAR 7)"**. Also `Fecha del aviso`. Directly relevant since Carta's core case is a missed SAR 7. |

## Still missing

**The Spanish SAR 7 form itself (SAR 7 SP).** Neither of these carries the required-document
checklist, so the `doc_types` translations — pay stubs, rent receipt, utility bill, proof of
identity — are still unsourced.

Try: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/forms-brochures/translated-forms-and-publications/spanish
and look for SAR 7 under Spanish. Also worth grabbing **SAR 7A (SP)**, the "how to fill out
