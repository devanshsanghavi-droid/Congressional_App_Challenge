# Carta — demo video script

**About 2:50.** SPEC §12.1 allows 1–3 minutes. 365 written words, about 378 once
numbers are said aloud. With the written-in holds and a breath between sections
that is **2:43 at a brisk 160 words a minute, 2:50 at 150, 2:59 at a slow 140.**
Time one real read-through before recording; if it runs past 2:52, use the
optional cuts at the end.

Revised 2026-09-29 through three rounds of simulated judging and three
adversarial fact-checks (JOURNAL.md §9 has the numbers). Your two opening
sentences are unchanged. Order: the statistic, why it matters here, what Carta
does, the form check (the one big visual), what happens after a deadline and
before a letter, the testing, the accuracy, the close. No feature is presented
as new.

"In California, households are six times more likely to leave CalFresh, the

## 0:37 – 0:56 · The deadline, remembered

"Carta reads the letter on the phone, strips out the Social Security number, and

# Staging notes (the shots will not happen by themselves)

- **Fresh install, clear the prompts first.** iOS asks for the camera on the first
  photo and for notifications on the first Review save. Do one throwaway capture
  and save, answer both, delete that letter, then film the unbroken take.
- **The countdown and the lock-screen reminder.** The ladder only fires 30, 14, 7,
  3, 1 and 0 days before a deadline, at 9:00 AM. The reminder-time picker still
  shows "Unimplemented" on iOS, so the hour cannot be changed. Print a re-dated
  copy of a fictional letter due exactly 14 days after the shoot, confirm it the
  day before, and film the lock screen at 9:00. Long-press it open so the papers
  show.
- **"Did it come?".** Carta asks on the 8th of the sixth month after a SAR 7's due
  date. For the card to appear on the shoot day, confirm a re-dated fictional SAR 7
  whose due date is April 5, 2026 or earlier.
- **The form check has never run through a phone camera.** Before the shoot day,
  print `tools/forms/demo/sar7-demo-blank.pdf`, fill it in by hand with all four
  faults (question 2 blank, question 3 both boxes, no signature, a date inside the
  report month), and rehearse on the phone in flat, even light. Write the date in
  large, clear digits. If the date ring comes out grey (unreadable), type the date
  into the app on camera. Use a jump cut between fixing the form and reshooting.
- **The planted network call.** Re-create it for the shot: add
  `fetch('https://example.com')` inside `daysUntil` in `src/lib/urgency.ts`, film
  the privacy test failing red, remove it, film it green. Do not commit it.
- **The test count.** Record the voice-over and film the terminal on the same day,
  after the October 12 freeze, and read the number off that run. It will not stay
  752 if tests are added.
- **Spanish.** Show Home in Spanish, never "In plain words" on a Spanish letter (it
  answers in English).

# Do not say, and why

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| "Carta checks your SAR 7", or any SAR 7 | **Only the demonstration copy has a template**, and the script says so aloud. The county's layout needs its own template. |
| "the family confirms every detail" | Review confirms the dates, names and fields; it does **not** show the list of papers, which comes from the letter unconfirmed. The script says "every date and name". |
| "the reminder names the form" | It names the programme ("CalFresh: 14 days left") and says "Send the form back… Send: [papers]". |
| "Social Security numbers are never saved" | The redaction covers the **saved text** (8 formats, tested). The letter photo is deleted after reading by default. Say "stripped from the saved text". |
| "96.9% accurate" or "96.9% of its answers" | 96.9% is the share of **key details it filled in** that were right, on the letters it was built with. Across all fields it is 95.3%. |
| "three letters it had never seen" | **Two.** The third held-out notice was never photographed. On those two, 6 of 6 key details it filled in were right (6 of 7 until the case-number fix of 2026-09-29, disclosed in the README), and 6 of 12 were found. |
| "79 real scans" | 23 real photos, 56 made blurry, noisy, dark or compressed on purpose. Say "79 recorded scans". |
| "every test was proven by breaking it" | Specific ones were: the privacy test, the SSN tests, the case-number tests, the countdown cap, the timezone pin. |
| "I wrote 752 tests" | The README discloses the code, tests included, was written substantially with AI assistance. Say "Carta runs 752 tests". |
| "Vietnamese", "Chinese", "the state's own translation" | Not built. |
| "verified on a phone" for the form check, the new dates, the forecast or the hand-off | Simulator only until the staging rehearsal above happens. The hand-off has never crossed between two phones, which is why the script leaves it out. |
| "47 percent of Californians" | Survey respondents dropped from Medi-Cal: 47% in Month 1, 46% over six months. Say "nearly half of the people surveyed". |
| "Carta forecasts Medi-Cal letters" | Only the CalFresh renewal notice after a six-month report. |
| "what a chatbot can't" | Chatbots have memory and reminders now. The script says "doesn't". |
| "the database is encrypted" | Field-level only. |
| "1.3 seconds to first token" | Stale. Measured on an iPhone: 0.7–1.1 s to the first word. |

# Held in reserve

For written answers or Q&A, not the video. All measured, all in the repo; JOURNAL.md §12 has the full breakdown.

- 752 tests in 28 suites: 597 in bare Node and 155 in a simulated iPhone environment that renders the screens. CI re-runs all of them, plus typecheck and lint, on a clean Ubuntu machine. The whole suite takes about 3 seconds on the development Mac.
- The privacy test scans every source file in the app except one, 82 files, each as its own test. The one it skips, by name, is the optional model download. It replays 79 recorded scans with every network API booby-trapped.
- 42 tests exist only to prove the other tests can fail or are not passing on nothing.
- Three bugs found by auditing the claims made about the suite, all fixed with tests: SSN removal asserted for only 2 of 8 formats; a masked SSN offered as a case number on Social Security letters; and a privacy test that never looked at the screens.
- Every accuracy figure is reported beside the OCR ceiling, and the letters the code was built with are never merged with the ones it never saw.
- Date arithmetic runs in local calendar days, because `(a - b) / 86400000` returns 90.04 days across a daylight-saving change.

# Do not film

- **Settings → "Remind me at."** `RNDateTimePicker` resolves to an unimplemented placeholder on iOS.
- **A Spanish notice through "In plain words".** It answers in English.
- **The model download.** 1.12 GB, minutes of nothing.
- **Two phones doing the hand-off**, until it has actually worked on two phones.
- **A real county SAR 7 in the form check.** It will say it cannot check it.

# Verify before publishing

- **25 million and 69 percent.** KFF's Medicaid Enrollment and Unwinding Tracker. The 2026-09-29 fact-checker reported that the tracker gives "over 25 million" disenrolled and 69% for procedural reasons. That was an AI checker's reading: open the tracker yourself once.
- **Six times; more than half likely still eligible.** California Policy Lab, on CalFresh exits (households).
- **Nearly half never got a renewal form.** DHCS Medi-Cal Disenrollment Survey: 47% in Month 1, 46% across all six months.
