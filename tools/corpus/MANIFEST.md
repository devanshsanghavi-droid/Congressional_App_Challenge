## Synthetic degradation (56)

In `synthetic/`, generated by `degrade.py`, catalogued in `synthetic_manifest.json`.

### Why these exist

**iPhone computational photography sharpens text after capture.** Deep Fusion and Smart HDR detect document text and repair blur, so genuine motion blur is effectively uncapturable with the stock camera app — every attempt came back near-sharp. `cf3776-blur-11.jpg` is the evidence: it was shot as a blur test and the pipeline fixed it.

Blur and sensor noise are therefore generated in software with fixed, documented parameters. **Everything else in the corpus — skew, crease, shadow, low light — is a real physical capture**, because those the phone does not undo.

### Variants — 7 per source × 8 clean sources

| Tag | Parameters |
|---|---|
| `synth-motionblur` | 15px linear kernel, 0° |
| `synth-motionblur-diag` | 21px linear kernel, 35° — hardest in the corpus |
| `synth-defocus` | Gaussian radius 2.2px |
| `synth-defocus-heavy` | Gaussian radius 4.0px |
| `synth-noise` | Gaussian luminance noise, σ=9 |
| `synth-underexposed` | gamma 1.75 + σ=7 noise |
| `synth-jpeg` | JPEG quality 22 recompression |

Deterministic **within a Pillow build** — seed 20261026, fixed kernels. Across Pillow versions it is not: resampling and JPEG encoding differ, and blur variants shift materially. Committed set was generated with **Pillow 12.3.0**. Measured aggregate impact of a rebuild: ±3pp per condition.

### Scoring rule

**Report real and synthetic as separate buckets. Never merge them into one headline accuracy number.** The honest claim is:

> 23 real captures across 5 physical degradation conditions, plus 56 synthetically degraded variants across 7 parameterized conditions, scored separately.

## Notes for the harness

- Shot on a granite counter — busy, high-contrast background. Realistic, and it exercises page-boundary detection. Don't crop it out.
- Real captures are **2000×2666** on disk (downscaled from 12MP at HEIC conversion). Synthetic variants are 1700px wide. The harness normalises both to 1700px so resolution is never confounded with degradation.
- **`sar7-clean-01`, `-dim-02`, `-angled-03`, `-shadow-05`, `-creased-04` are the same physical sheet under five conditions with identical ground truth.** Any accuracy delta across those five is purely the condition. That's the cleanest measurement available and it belongs in the README.

## Reshoot note — notice 02 (2026-08-18)

The three `na960x-*` captures were reshot after the chronology fix. All three show the
corrected notice (notice date SEPTEMBER 8, 2026; aid-paid-pending SEPTEMBER 18, 2026).

Two are not the condition their filename implies, and the condition table should reflect
what they actually are:

- **`na960x-dim-07`** is a **strong magenta/purple colour cast** under LED accent
  lighting, at normal brightness — not low light. This is a legitimately hard and
  realistic condition (people photograph mail under whatever lighting is on), but it is a
  *different variable* than the dim captures of SAR 7 and MC 210. Score it as its own
  condition, `colour-cast`, or the low-light bucket becomes incoherent.

- **`na960x-angled-08`** is rotated roughly **180° plus perspective skew** — the text is
  upside down. That makes it an *orientation* test, not a skew test. It's valuable
  (orientation detection is a real failure mode and EXIF won't save you here) but it
  should not sit in the same bucket as `sar7-angled-03`, which is skew at upright
  orientation. Suggest condition `inverted`.

Net effect: notice 02 now contributes `flat`, `colour-cast`, and `inverted`, and no
longer contributes to the `dim` or `angled` buckets. That is a gain in coverage, not a
loss — but only if the labels are honest.

## Statistical caveat — small-n conditions

Several conditions have n=1 or n=2:

| condition | n |
|---|---|
| colour-cast | 1 |
| inverted | 1 |
| dim-angled | 1 |
| blur-attempt | 1 |
| shadow | 2 |

**A percentage on a single image is not a measurement.** "colour-cast: 100%" means one
photo worked, not that colour casts are solved. Report these as raw counts (`9/9 fields
on 1 image`), never as a rate, and don't put them in a chart that invites comparison
against `flat` (n=8) or `creased` (n=5).

The conditions with enough images to support a claim are **flat (8)**, **creased (5)**,
**dim (3)**, and **angled (3)**. Everything else is an existence proof: evidence the
pipeline doesn't fall over, not evidence about a rate.
